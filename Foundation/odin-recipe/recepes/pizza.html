Odin Recipes Pizza

Description

Pizza is a popular Italian dish made with a soft dough base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and a variety of vegetables or other toppings. It is baked until the crust is crisp and the cheese is melted.

Homemade pizza can be customized with your favorite toppings and is a great choice for a quick and enjoyable meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon dry yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup warm water
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ½ cup chopped capsicum
  • ½ cup sliced tomato
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • ½ teaspoon chilli flakes

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Add warm water and olive oil, then mix to form a dough.
  3. Knead the dough for about 5–10 minutes until it becomes smooth.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rest for about 1 hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  6. Roll the dough into a round pizza base.
  7. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the base.
  8. Add mozzarella cheese, onion, capsicum, and tomato.
  9. Sprinkle oregano and chilli flakes over the toppings.
  10. Bake the pizza for about 12–15 minutes until the cheese melts and the crust becomes golden.
  11. Slice the pizza and serve hot.
