Odin Recipes khichdi

  • 1 cup rice
  • ½ cup yellow moong dal
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 small tomato, chopped
  • small carrot, chopped
  • 1 small potato, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 tablespoon ghee or oil
  • –5 cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 small piece of ginger, grated
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnishing

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and moong dal thoroughly and soak them in water for about 15–20 minutes.
  2. Heat ghee or oil in a pressure cooker and add cumin seeds.
  3. Once the cumin seeds start to crackle, add the chopped onion and sauté until it becomes slightly golden.
  4. Add ginger, tomato, carrot, and potato, and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Add turmeric powder, red chilli powder, and salt. Mix everything well.
  6. Drain the soaked rice and dal, then add them to the cooker.
  7. Add 4–5 cups of water and mix well.
  8. Close the pressure cooker and cook for about 3–4 whistles on medium heat.
  9. Allow the pressure to release naturally, then open the cooker and mix the khichdi well.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with ghee, curd, pickle, or papad.
